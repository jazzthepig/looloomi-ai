## 2. Strategy research — the findings (all from our own data, benchmark-relative)

**A. The CIS signal is monotonically predictive of 30-day alpha** (it ranks assets correctly):

| Signal | N | Avg 30d alpha | Alpha win |
|---|---|---|---|
| STRONG OUTPERFORM | 134 | **+3.32%** | 50% |
| OUTPERFORM | 1,475 | −0.37% | 38% |
| UNDERWEIGHT | 243 | −1.18% | 42% |
| UNDERPERFORM | 4,376 | **−1.79%** | 36% |

Two edges: a **long** edge (top tier) and a **short** edge (UNDERPERFORM, large sample). The
aggregate looked flat because the noise tier drowns the signal tier.

**B. The edge is REGIME-CONDITIONAL** (which end pays flips with the regime):
- RISK_ON → long STRONG OUTPERFORM (+6.5% alpha). Short fails.
- RISK_OFF → short UNDERPERFORM (asset −3.6% → short +3.6%, N=3,202). Long has ~0 signals.
- EASING → both fail; stand aside.

**C. Continuous RISK GRADIENT** (benchmark trailing 30d return) sharpens it — the edge is
monotonic along the gradient, not a 6-label switch:
- deep-off → short laggards +6.0%; long leaders negative.
- deep-on → long leaders **+10.5%** (100d CIS) / **+26.8%** (11yr momentum-proxy backtest);
  short gets squeezed (−5 to −6%).

**D. 11-YEAR backtest (2015→2026, our OHLCV, momentum proxy of the signal) CONFIRMS the structure**
across multiple cycles: momentum-leaders' forward alpha runs −6% (deep-off) → +4.5% (on) → +26.8%
(deep-on), monotonic. The long-leaders-in-risk-on edge is decade-persistent, not a sample fluke.

**E. Per-asset:** the long edge concentrates — **ETH** fires 46 STRONG OUTPERFORM at **+13.8% alpha**
(most of any asset), LINK/ARB/LDO also. Most assets rarely fire the top tier. **HYPE**: too new
(<20 matured signals). Per-asset SHORT (UNDERPERFORM) has good N (~180/asset); per-asset LONG is
thin except ETH/LINK.

**Implications:** (1) size STRONG OUTPERFORM + A/A+ up, plain OUTPERFORM as watchlist; (2) make
conviction **regime-conditional** (long-top in risk-on, short-bottom in risk-off) — but N-gated,
data earns it; (3) product: a Glassnode-tier "risk-gradient × tier expected-alpha" map — a
decision surface, every cell traced to real outcomes.

**Discipline (大象无形):** the signal's *ranking* is stable; the *tradeable direction* is regime-
conditional. You cannot grab it as a static factor. Backtests validate hypotheses; never trade a
frozen factor. Don't overfit thin regime/asset cells (N-gate everything).
