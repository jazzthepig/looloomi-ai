Issuer xStocks are issued by Backed Assets (JE) Limited, a Jersey-based special purpose vehicle dedicated solely to the issuance and redemption of xStocks. The SPV maintains its own bank and collateral accounts and conducts KYC and AML procedures in accordance with Jersey law. Backed Assets (JE) Limited is registered with the Jersey Financial Services Commission (JFSC) and holds the required COBO and CGPO consents to issue security tokens. Legal Classification Each xStock is a bearer debt instrument classified as a tracker certificate. It provides economic exposure to the underlying equity. It does not confer shareholder voting rights. xStocks are structured financial instruments, not direct equity ownership. Prospectus & Distribution For distribution in the EU/EEA, the products are governed by a base prospectus approved by the Liechtenstein Financial Market Authority (FMA) under the EU Prospectus Regulation. This approval is passportable across the European Economic Area, enabling licensed third-party distributors to offer xStocks to retail clients. Each individual product is governed by its own Final Terms published alongside the base prospectus. Although xStocks are issued as securities under the frameworks of Jersey and the European Union, other jurisdictions may classify them differently, including as crypto assets. Licensing requirements for distribution vary accordingly. Product documentation, including the prospectus and Final Terms, is available at: assets.backed.fi/legal-documentation Collateral & Segregation Every xStock is fully collateralized by the corresponding underlying asset. Collateral is held with regulated custodians and brokers. Each product is backed on an asset-by-asset basis. Dedicated sub-accounts are used to ensure segregation. No commingling of collateral between products occurs. This means tokens referencing one equity are backed solely by that specific equity. Safeguarding & Bankruptcy Protection Collateral accounts are governed by a three-party Account Control Agreement between the issuer, the custodian or broker, and an independent Security Agent. The Security Agent has visibility over collateral accounts and may assume control if token holders' rights, as defined in the prospectus, are not being upheld. In the event of issuer default, the Security Agent may take control of the collateral accounts, liquidate the underlying assets, and distribute proceeds to token holders in accordance with the prospectus terms. Blockchain Registration Ownership of xStocks is recorded on public blockchains. The Swiss DLT Act establishes that DLT-based records are valid evidence of title to financial instruments, providing legal certainty for this form of registration. The token is the certificate. Holding the token constitutes holding the instrument. Distribution Restrictions xStocks are not marketed, offered, or solicited in the United States, to U.S. Persons, or in any other prohibited jurisdiction.
