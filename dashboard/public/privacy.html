Privacy Policy

CometCloud AI (Looloomi Ltd) · Last updated: May 2026

CometCloud AI ("we", "us", "our") operates the platform at looloomi.ai and related services. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our platform, API, or MCP server.

1. Information We Collect

API key registration: When you request an API key, we collect your name, email address, and intended use. This information is stored in our database (Supabase) and used solely for key management and service communications.

Lead capture: If you submit a contact form or inquiry on our platform, we collect your name, email, organization, and message. We use this to respond to inquiries and, with your consent, send relevant updates.

Wallet authentication: If you connect a Solana wallet, we store your public wallet address and authentication nonce. We never have access to private keys or signing authority.

Usage data: We log API requests (endpoint, timestamp, key prefix, response status) for rate limiting, abuse prevention, and service improvement. We do not log request bodies or response payloads.

Cookies and analytics: Our platform uses minimal session data. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or sell user data to advertisers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the API service and enforce rate limits
  • To respond to support requests and inquiries
  • To send service-critical communications (key expiry, policy changes)
  • To improve our scoring models and platform features
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL, hosted in the EU). API keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes — the raw key is shown only once at creation and never stored in plaintext. We use industry-standard TLS encryption for all data in transit.

Access to production databases is restricted to authorized personnel. We apply row-level security (RLS) on all user-facing Supabase tables.

4. Data Retention

API keys remain active until revoked or until 12 months of inactivity. Score history and analytics data is retained for up to 90 days in our primary database. Usage logs are retained for 30 days. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

5. Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with the following third-party services, each subject to their own privacy policies:

  • CoinGecko — market price data (no personal data shared)
  • DeFiLlama — DeFi TVL data (no personal data shared)
  • Supabase — database hosting (EU region)
  • Upstash — Redis cache (no personal data stored)
  • Railway — backend hosting (no personal data beyond server logs)

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@cometcloud.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Hong Kong, additional rights may apply under GDPR, UK GDPR, or the PDPO respectively.

7. Investment Information Disclaimer

CometCloud AI provides market intelligence and scoring signals for informational purposes only. Nothing on this platform constitutes investment advice, a solicitation to buy or sell securities, or a recommendation to enter any transaction. All signals use positioning language (OUTPERFORM, NEUTRAL, UNDERPERFORM) and do not constitute buy or sell recommendations. Past performance of any scoring model does not guarantee future results.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified by email to registered API users or posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
