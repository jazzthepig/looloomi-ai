Structural advantages

Four things you can't replicate without all four pieces

Local inference at institutional scale
Mac Mini M4 Pro with Gemma 27B runs 24/7. No API cost ceiling. Macro briefs, signal synthesis, and pattern recognition run locally — so we can analyze 84 assets every 30 minutes without a $50k/month inference bill.
Cloud inference: ~$0.01/call × 84 assets × 48 pushes/day = $40/day
Local inference: $0/call. Hardware paid once.
Regime-aware scoring, not point-in-time
Most scoring systems are regime-agnostic — they score an asset the same way in TIGHTENING as in EASING. We adjust pillar weights dynamically: in Tightening, Fundamental and On-chain dominate; in Risk-On, Momentum and Alpha dominate.
TIGHTENING: F×35% M×20% O×25% S×10% A×10%
RISK_ON: F×20% M×30% O×15% S×20% A×15%
On-chain fundamental data TradFi can't see
DeFiLlama TVL flows, protocol revenue, staking dynamics, and DeFi yield rates are inputs to the Fundamental pillar. A protocol losing $500M TVL in 7 days gets penalized in F score — this appears in Bloomberg 2 weeks later as "outflow." We catch it when it happens.
